1. **Tail truncation in `fit()` drops `#immigration` and corrupts the URL (plus unescaped `#` in titles)** — [community.ts:58](file:///Users/jeong-ilin/study/dailybit/src/lib/immigration/community.ts#L58), [community.ts:110](file:///Users/jeong-ilin/study/dailybit/src/lib/immigration/community.ts#L110)
   `fit()` slices the end of the already-joined note (`[title, summary, url, #immigration]`). Because `SUMMARY_MAX` is 300 ([community-ai.ts:8](file:///Users/jeong-ilin/study/dailybit/src/lib/immigration/community-ai.ts#L8)), the article URL is ~81 chars, and `a.title` has no length cap in `parseCndreamsList` ([community.ts:28](file:///Users/jeong-ilin/study/dailybit/src/lib/immigration/community.ts#L28)), any note over 500 chars has `#immigration` and part of `a.url` sliced off the tail—so `parseTags(content)` ([store.ts:22](file:///Users/jeong-ilin/study/dailybit/src/lib/immigration/store.ts#L22)) saves the Bit without the `immigration` tag. Also, unlike `summary` ([community-ai.ts:69](file:///Users/jeong-ilin/study/dailybit/src/lib/immigration/community-ai.ts#L69)), `a.title` does not escape `#` to `＃`, so `#word` in a headline becomes an unintended tag.

2. **Failed or empty article body fetch sends the headline to Claude as the article body** — [community.ts:89-98](file:///Users/jeong-ilin/study/dailybit/src/lib/immigration/community.ts#L89-L98)
   When `fetchText(item.url)` throws or `articleBody()` returns `""` (e.g. `#news_content_id` markup change), `a.body` is `""`. Line 98 passes `body: a.body || a.title` to `summarize()`, prompting Claude ([community-ai.ts:16](file:///Users/jeong-ilin/study/dailybit/src/lib/immigration/community-ai.ts#L16)) to write a 2–3 sentence summary from the title alone instead of falling back to title + link, and then marks the article as seen ([community.ts:115-116](file:///Users/jeong-ilin/study/dailybit/src/lib/immigration/community.ts#L115-L116)).

3. **Unparsed article date (`date === ""`) on first run silently marks articles as seen without summarizing** — [community.ts:34](file:///Users/jeong-ilin/study/dailybit/src/lib/immigration/community.ts#L34), [community.ts:81](file:///Users/jeong-ilin/study/dailybit/src/lib/immigration/community.ts#L81), [community.ts:115](file:///Users/jeong-ilin/study/dailybit/src/lib/immigration/community.ts#L115)
   If the date regex on line 34 fails to match, `date` defaults to `""`. On `firstRun`, `i.date >= from` (`"" >= "YYYY-MM-DD"`) is `false`, so those articles are excluded from both `candidates` and `deferred`, and line 115 permanently records their `idx` in `saveSeen`.

4. **Corrupt `PageSnapshot.text` returns `[]` instead of `null`, bypassing the 14-day first-run window** — [store.ts:75-80](file:///Users/jeong-ilin/study/dailybit/src/lib/immigration/store.ts#L75-L80), [community.ts:76-81](file:///Users/jeong-ilin/study/dailybit/src/lib/immigration/community.ts#L76-L81)
   If `row.text` is invalid JSON or not an array, `getSeen()` returns `[]` rather than `null` (or throwing). `runCommunityDigest` then sets `firstRun = false` with an empty `seen` set, so `!firstRun || i.date >= from` matches every historical article on the list page regardless of age.
